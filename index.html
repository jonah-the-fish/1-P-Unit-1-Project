Who is Norma Desmond?

Norma Desmond is the main antagonist of Billy Wilder's 1950 film Sunset Boulevard. She is a former movie star who has faded into a reclusive lifestyle, but constantly preparing a grand return. The film focuses on her fragile mental state and eventual descent into madness brought about by a torrid love affair with young writer Joe Gillis.
